# ZeroReflections — proposed ADR-012 extension

**Status:** Concept proposal.

Optional private journaling prompts after a user-authorized calendar event, shared plan or possible meaningful moment. Example: “What would you like to remember about today?” Do not infer that two people met simply because devices were near each other.

Reflections may link to a person, place, event and selected photos, but the user writes their meaning. Never manufacture dialogue, emotions or relationship conclusions. Manual entry is always possible. Support snooze, quiet hours, disable, person/place/date exclusions and prompt frequency limits.

Keep private reflections locally encrypted by default; sharing requires explicit selection and recipient confirmation where applicable. Provide portable Markdown/JSON export with provenance and deletion controls. “On this day” resurfacing must be opt-in and sensitive to exclusions.
